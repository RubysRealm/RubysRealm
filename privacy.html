RubysRealm Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RubysRealm handles information when users access or use the service.

Information We Access

When a user connects a TikTok account, RubysRealm may access basic account information and permissions that the user authorizes through TikTok.

How Information Is Used

Authorized information is used to provide content creation, upload, and publishing functionality requested by the user.

TikTok Integration

RubysRealm may communicate with TikTok services to provide account authorization, content uploading, and content publishing functionality.

Sharing of Information

RubysRealm does not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to TikTok when necessary to provide TikTok-related functionality requested and authorized by the user.

User Control

Users maintain control over their TikTok account and may revoke permissions granted to RubysRealm through their TikTok account settings.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information associated with the service.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as RubysRealm develops, its functionality changes, or applicable requirements change.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be submitted through the RubysRealm service.
